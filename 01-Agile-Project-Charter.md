# 📜 Agile Project Charter

## 1. Project Vision & Value Proposition
**Vision:** To bridge the digital divide in Khayelitsha by providing affordable, reliable, uncapped home internet to residents and small business owners.
**Value Proposition:** Our business helps residents in Khayelitsha who struggle with a lack of affordable internet by providing a competitively priced fixed wireless service (R399/month) so that they can access education, job opportunities, and entertainment without exceeding their household budgets.

## 2. Project Scope & Objectives
**In Scope:**
*   Procuring a high-speed fibre link.
*   Installing small wireless dishes at customer homes.
*   Providing a 10 Mbps uncapped internet package.
*   Targeting residents currently paying R409 - R799 for DSTV and mobile data.

**Out of Scope:**
*   Building physical fibre-to-the-home (FTTH) infrastructure.
*   Providing hardware (laptops/phones) to residents.

## 4. Problem Validation & Evidence (Appendix A Summary)
We conducted informal interviews with 17 households in Khayelitsha to validate our business idea.
*   **Total Interviews:** 17 households
*   **Willingness to pay for affordable internet:** 14 out of 17 (82%)
*   **High frustration levels with current services:** 14 out of 17 (82%)
*   **Key Insight:** Many households pay R409 to R799 for satellite TV and expensive mobile data. They are highly willing to switch to a R399 uncapped internet service that provides better value.
