## Reflection
Compiling the Product Backlog taught me the importance of prioritization. We had many ideas, but I had to critically evaluate which tasks were essential for the business to function (High Priority: Fibre Setup, Customer Payment) versus tasks that could wait (Low Priority: Website Development). **My role** involved adding detailed descriptions and checklists to the cards we created. I learned that the Product Backlog is never truly "finished"—as we learned more about the community's needs, we had to adapt and refine the items. This process demonstrated that Agile is about responding to change, not just following a rigid list.
