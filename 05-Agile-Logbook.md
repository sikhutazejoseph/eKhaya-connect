## Reflection
The Agile Logbook was perhaps the most eye-opening part of the project. Writing down our daily standups forced me to confront our bottlenecks honestly. I recorded that some team members were initially unfamiliar with Trello, and that coordinating schedules for practice was difficult. **My role** included updating the logbook daily and helping teammates overcome their Trello struggles. Reflecting on this daily taught me that communication is the most critical part of Agile. By tracking our impediments, we were able to solve them as a team, which ultimately resulted in a successful and confident presentation.
